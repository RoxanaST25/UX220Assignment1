## Research

While comparing my own portfolio goals, like keeping track of my progress, showing off my best work, and feeling more confident about my future career with the academic learning outcomes from the article “Portfolios and E-Portfolios: Student Reflection, Self-Assessment, and Goal Setting in the Learning Process”, I noticed some similarities and differences. 

Like my personal goals and the article's academic outcomes are all about reflecting on what I've learned and taking charge of my own education. However, my personal goals are usually smaller and more specific, like organizing my weekly assignments or getting thorough the weekly lessons, achieving good grades, etc; while academic learning outcomes look at the big picture of meeting school standards and building critical thinking skills. Even though they look a bit different, both point to the same outcome of a portfolio providing the ability of looking back and seeing how much you've grown, and using this to progress to the next level.


Reference:

Belgrad, S. (2013). Portfolios and E-Portfolios: Student Reflection. Self-Assessment, and Goal Setting in the Learning Process. In SAGE Handbook of Research on Classroom Assessment (pp. 331–346).
